## Step 1 — philosophy and positioning (OWNER approved)

**OWNER APPROVED:** Intensjonsdrevet SEO as philosophy; kommersiell søkemotoroptimalisering as positioning; søkemarkedsdekning as the distinctive analytical perspective; "Fra søkemarked til ordrebok" as the six-step methodology. The buyer must learn why the approach works through concrete mechanisms, relevant comparisons, demonstrations and evidence. Show the benefits rather than merely claiming superiority. Purchase intent and business value guide prioritization without excluding valuable informational demand. No unverified claim of market exclusivity or guaranteed outcomes. "Det er tanken som teller" remains a creative candidate, not approved Hero wording.

OWNER wants to explore Edward Sturm's Compact Keywords / bottom-of-funnel philosophy and differentiate from generic volume-first SEO. The working hook "Det er tanken som teller" is an OWNER-suggested creative experiment, not approved Hero copy. The six-step "Fra søkemarked til ordrebok" method is approved strategic calibration and saved in BusinessIntelligence backlog 449; its final expression remains open.

Recommended positioning for discussion: start with the commercial opportunity in the search market, not aggregate traffic. Identify what customers intend to accomplish, which services/products are economically worthwhile, where the business can win, and what page must satisfy the intent. Prioritize underserved purchase/use/contact intent without excluding valuable supporting informational content. Execute the selected work and measure outcomes where data permits. Differentiation is the combination of intent, opportunity mapping, implementation and measurement, not an unverified assertion that no other Norwegian agency does this.

Audience hypothesis: businesses competing for customers where relevant visibility and qualified demand have real economic value; not necessarily every small business. No promises of rankings, new sales, or product availability beyond actual offer and evidence. Validate current system capabilities before public claims about SeoTool/LeadProof.

Decision recorded above. Section structure approved in step 2; no final Hero approved.

## Step 1 — alternative concept exploration

OWNER prefers the combination of intent-led philosophy and commercial positioning. Keep "Fra søkemarked til ordrebok" as the methodology. OWNER asks to explore competing/complementary concepts from the knowledge bank before closing step 1 and wants the eventual page to teach the buyer why the approach is better suited to commercial outcomes, through concrete mechanisms and comparisons rather than unsupported superiority claims.

Research candidates (strategic concepts, not approved public copy):
- Revenue-first SEO: begin with products/services, margins, customer value and actual demand, then select queries and pages (Backlog 215, 223 [VURDER], 243, 364, 444).
- Search-market coverage: map commercially relevant demand clusters to owned pages and current visibility, discover uncovered needs and opportunities in existing pages; no false percentage of the whole market from incomplete GSC data (215, 362–364, 440, 444).
- Commercial pages as authority assets: build supporting authority around valuable service/product pages, rather than selling article counts (242–245, 248).
- First-party expertise as a competitive moat: use jobs, sales questions, support, original data and experience to create information a competitor cannot reproduce from the same generic prompt (260 reproducibility, 380, 382).
- Outcome-led measurement: distinguish visibility, qualified demand and actual business outcomes; separate branded/non-branded demand where available, use paid-search evidence when useful, and respect attribution coverage (75, 205–206, 284, 294).

Recommendation for decision: keep intent-led philosophy + commercial positioning as the umbrella; make search-market coverage the distinctive teachable mechanism and the six-step method the delivery model. First-party expertise and outcome-led measurement support it; no need to invent a new branded SEO term. Later sections should explain a concrete mechanism, example or comparison that lets the buyer understand why the priority matters, then connect it to what we do. Avoid repeated traffic-versus-orders rhetoric. Supporting education is a purpose, not a mandatory template for every section. OWNER has now confirmed the combined direction. Step 1 complete; alternatives retained as research history, not additional mandatory concepts.

## Buyer

Norwegian small and medium business owners seeking relevant enquiries and sales. They value understandable financial consequences, realistic delivery, direct buying choices and memorable explanations. Use their real situation and language.

## Brand point of view

- Clicks are not the end goal. Orders, qualified enquiries and profitable new customers matter.
- Sometimes improving the offer, page or measurement is more valuable than buying additional advertising.
- Challenge empty agency promises, unhelpful metrics and awkward customer journeys. Never ridicule business owners for unfamiliar terminology.
- Focus on the upside: more qualified leads, orders or better returns. Use a problem to reveal the opportunity, not as a catalogue of mistakes.
- An accessible financial or practical observation can open the door to precise professional explanations. Do not flatten useful technical depth.
- A persuasive worked example or Hero loses momentum when interrupted by unnecessary caveats. Explain material limitations and buying conditions where they matter; a labeled hypothetical calculation is not a client result.

## Creative principles

Find the overlooked mechanism, specific buyer consequence, genuine contrast or recognizable absurdity. A surprising idea is valuable when it makes a relevant point memorable. Let the actual research and concept determine the expression; never force percentages, short fragments or a comic ending merely because a previous example worked.

For high comedy, distinguish an earned setup, escalation and unexpected payoff, character dialogue or callback from a mildly cheeky observation. Originality and relevance matter more than a count of jokes.

## OWNER-approved examples

Source: existing Konvertering page, Antigravity-created copy identified by OWNER as desired creative calibration.

1. "Fra 1 til 2 % høres lite ut. Helt til bestillingene dobles."
   - Why it works: a tiny-sounding change becomes an obvious financial outcome.
   - Transferable principle: reveal an underestimated mechanism with a substantial economic consequence. Do not force percentage arithmetic into unrelated pages.
2. "Byråer elsker å selge mer trafikk. Vi foretrekker elementær barneskolematte som fyller kontoen din."
   - Why it works: a sharp brand viewpoint, commercial critique and tangible alternative.
   - Transferable principle: challenge a genuine industry assumption and offer a credible better priority. Do not mimic the two-sentence pattern.

### OWNER-approved Spiss+ calibration (nivå 4, 2026-10-22)

FAQ question: **Hva hvis både synlighet og konvertering begrenser resultatet?**

> Da finner vi ut om kundene ikke finner deg, eller om de finner deg og gir opp. Å skaffe mer trafikk til en nettside uten en tydelig vei til kontakt, er som å annonsere for en butikk som har gjemt inngangen. Flere interesserte. Samme problem.

This **tone reference** was explicitly preferred by OWNER over the preceding `Spiss` example. It is **not** approval to replace or publish the existing global FAQ answer, and it must not be repeated as a template on every card or page.

Why it works: starts with a concrete diagnostic fork, uses a real-world analogy for wasted traffic, then lands the commercial cost in a dry final observation. The factual answer remains understandable without the analogy. For highly sensitive, exact, legal or proof-heavy topics, tone is subdued regardless of the selected preset.

## Experiments and historical illustrations (not independently approved)

- Google Ads page concept: "Google Ads skal skaffe kunder. Klikk har du allerede betalt for."
  - Why it may work: directly states the service, buyer outcome and frustration.
  - Principle: contrast the purchased input with the outcome the buyer actually wants. Test candidate, not an independently OWNER-approved lock.
- Fictional high-comedy sketch, not a client case: 'En kunde vil bare vite hva en nettside koster. Skjemaet spør om navn, telefon, mors pikenavn og hvor han var natt til 14. juli. Nå har han advokat. Fortsatt ingen pris.'
  - Principle: escalate a recognizable friction point to an unexpected but logically connected payoff. This is internal fiction, not an approved production line or a scene to repeat.

## OWNER-rejected examples

Source: OWNER feedback, 2026-11-03, Lokal SEO Hero. Jokes about Skatteetaten, postbudet and rørlegger-etterlysning were rejected. Exact lines are not reproduced here; the documented reason was that slightly cheeky is not Hysterisk. A funny authority, police reference or Chuck Norris mention is not itself a joke.

Transferable lesson: a real comedic premise and payoff must earn the humor. A smile can qualify as level 5/6; level 7 requires a stronger comic mechanism and cannot be proved by a model self-rating. Do not recycle these rejected executions.

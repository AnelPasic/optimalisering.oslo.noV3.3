# Creative DNA - Optimalisering Oslo

Status: Site-specific editorial calibration. The actual saved presets remain in `coordination/CONTENT-TONE.json`; copy rules remain in `coordination/COPY-STYLE.md`. This file protects original, owner-liked wording; it is not a CMS setting or permission to rewrite existing pages.

## Buyer
Norwegian small and medium business owners who want more relevant enquiries and sales. Many know very little about SEO, quality score, Ads bidding or CRO. They need understandable financial consequences, realistic delivery, direct buying choices and memorable explanations.

## Point of view
- Clicks are not the end goal. An order, qualified enquiry or profitable new customer matters more.
- Sometimes the right advice is to improve the offer, web page or measurement before spending more on advertising.
- Challenge empty agency promises and marketing dashboards. Do not mock business owners for unfamiliar technical terms.
- Make a strong, concrete claim first. Explain the mechanism in everyday words. Sell the upside: more qualified leads, more orders, better return. Never turn the page into a list of losses or mistakes.
- Do not interrupt Hero, worked examples, sales sections or benefit cards with technical caveat paragraphs. A compact "Regneeksempel" label is enough for simple arithmetic. Place optional explanations in FAQ. Keep real-case dates, approved prices, ad-spend exclusions and other material buying conditions accurate and visible.
- Verify claims in proportion to risk. Basic advertising mechanics need a short authoritative check, not an exhaustive research report; client results, prices, promises and legal facts require approved evidence.

   Why: directly states the service, buyer outcome and frustration. This is a test candidate, not an independently owner-approved lock.

## Rules for creative writing
1. For new important sales content, consider genuinely different angles internally before choosing one. Reject irrelevant cleverness and filler.
2. Keep the buyer's job, desired upside and one economic consequence immediately understandable. The customer asks "Hvordan tjener jeg mer på dette?", not "Hvor taper jeg penger?". Use problems to set up the opportunity; put the reward at the center.
3. Use the current components, colored cards and approved layouts to reinforce the argument visually. No empty white stretches or new speculative design system.
4. Do not weaken an approved hook during copy QA. Fix punctuation, unclear syntax and factual overreach locally. If a fix would kill the actual insight, explain the conflict.
5. No made-up customer numbers, guarantee of 10x cheaper Ads, or false claim that SEO itself sets Google Ads quality score. Hypothetical math may be bold when clearly marked as hypothetical.
6. Run the available copy QA after authoring; do not assume the model remembers forbidden punctuation.

FAQ question: **Hva hvis både synlighet og konvertering begrenser resultatet?**

> Da finner vi ut om kundene ikke finner deg, eller om de finner deg og gir opp. Å skaffe mer trafikk til en nettside uten en tydelig vei til kontakt, er som å annonsere for en butikk som har gjemt inngangen. Flere interesserte. Samme problem.

This **tone reference** was explicitly preferred by OWNER over the preceding `Spiss` example. It is **not** approval to replace or publish the existing global FAQ answer, and it must not be repeated as a template on every card or page.

Why it works: starts with a concrete diagnostic fork, uses a real-world analogy for wasted traffic, then lands the commercial cost in a dry final observation. The factual answer remains understandable without the analogy. For highly sensitive, exact, legal or proof-heavy topics, tone is subdued regardless of the selected preset.

## Hysterisk quality calibration - internal fiction, not production copy

The OWNER rejected the 2026-11-03 Lokal SEO Hero jokes about Skatteetaten, postbudet and rørlegger-etterlysning: **slightly cheeky is not Hysterisk**. Never recycle these as passed examples. Mentioning a funny authority, police or Chuck Norris does not itself make a real joke.

- **Explore distinct mechanisms:** an invented mini-scene with escalating consequences and a sideways last line; sharp fictional character dialogue; absurd bureaucracy with internal logic; a surprise callback. A single dry comparison or one cheeky phrase is insufficient.
- **Fictional sketch (not a client case):** 'En kunde vil bare vite hva en nettside koster. Skjemaet spør om navn, telefon, mors pikenavn og hvor han var natt til 14. juli. Nå har han advokat. Fortsatt ingen pris.' Understand the setup/payoff, but do not copy this scene by default.
- **An explicitly comedic Hero** still needs full-Hero service/value clarity, but its humor must be substantive. Never append a polite joke after a generic SEO introduction and declare it 'hysterisk'.
- **Real evaluation:** have a person unaware of the prompt read it. A smile can qualify as level 5/6; level 7 is a higher editorial target, not something proved by model self-rating, repository tests or successful deployment.

## Core pricing logic

Do not sell the packages primarily as more hours.

The upgrade path should reflect increasing **problem complexity and simultaneity**:

### Optimalisering — 4 500 kr/mnd
**Ett viktig problem om gangen.**

Best fit:
- small/mid-sized business with one clear main customer journey or one dominant bottleneck;
- one main service/offer can be the typical case;
- examples may include a competitive local trades/service business, smaller clinic, accounting office, or niche B2B company.

Work model:
- sequential;
- one main bottleneck at a time;
- supporting work around the same commercial goal may be necessary;
- not intended to run two independent growth tracks in parallel.

Important anti-gaming principle:
> The package is not “the 6 900 package spread across alternating months”.
> If the result depends on two areas improving at the same time, Vekst is the better fit.

Visual direction:
- one upward arrow / one growth lever;
- no unsupported percentage claims.

### Vekst — 6 900 kr/mnd
**To grep som forsterker hverandre.**

Positioning:
- recommended until real sales data can truthfully support “Mest valgt”;
- do not use “Mest valgt” before it is actually true.

Best fit:
- business where two parts of the same commercial system should improve in parallel.

Primary examples:
1. Google Ads + konverteringsoptimalisering  
   Get relevant traffic now and improve how much value is created from each paid visit.
2. SEO/AI-synlighet + konverteringsoptimalisering  
   Build more relevant organic discovery and convert more of the visitors who arrive.
3. Google Ads + SEO/AI-synlighet  
   Use paid visibility for speed while building organic visibility that can reduce long-term dependence on ad spend / cost per acquired customer, especially when conversion already works reasonably well.

Core rule:
> Velg Vekst hvis resultatet avhenger av at to ting forbedres samtidig.

Work model:
- parallel;
- compound/reinforcing effects;
- should be explained as a different operating model from 4 500, not merely more work.

Visual direction:
- two arrows/levers that combine into a steeper curve;
- communicate reinforcement, not a guaranteed result.

### Partner — fra 14 900 kr/mnd
**Flere tjenester og kundereiser.**

Best fit:
- business with multiple distinct services, product areas, locations or audiences;
- each journey may have separate competitors, search demand, landing pages and economics.

Typical mental model:
- one company with several different commercial systems, e.g. canteen, catering, fruit delivery and other B2B services.

Work model:
- prioritize continuously across several customer journeys;
- different levers may apply to different journeys;
- not “everything all the time” and not unlimited.

Visual direction:
- multiple separate growth tracks / customer journeys;
- several curves/paths, clearly differentiated from Vekst’s two reinforcing levers.

### Optional high-end offer — not a fourth equal package card
**Ekstern markedsavdeling — from roughly 30–40k/month, exact price not locked**

Purpose:
- separate anchor/enterprise-style offer for a company wanting a larger share of ongoing marketing responsibility outsourced.

Potential responsibilities:
- prioritization;
- SEO;
- paid media;
- CRO;
- landing pages;
- campaigns;
- coordination;
- reporting.

Do not present this as “Partner+” or a fourth equal card unless later explicitly decided.

## Flagship logic: conversion optimization

CRO remains the flagship optimization lever because it can improve the value of traffic from several sources at once.

Core model:
> Resultat = relevant trafikk × konverteringsrate × kundeverdi

SEO/AI/local/Ads primarily affect relevant discovery/traffic.
CRO affects what happens after the visit and may therefore multiply the value of traffic from organic, paid and other sources.

## Cross-channel compound effect

Do not claim “SEO score makes Google Ads 10x cheaper”.

Safe commercial mechanism:
- the same underlying relevance/landing-page quality work can support organic discoverability;
- improve landing-page relevance/experience for paid traffic;
- improve conversion;
- therefore one improvement may create value in more than one part of the acquisition system.

Use Google Ads Quality Score only as a diagnostic/context concept, never as a guaranteed CPC multiplier.

## Multiplication visual

Simple educational example:
- 1 000 relevant visits × 2% = 20 actions
- 2 000 × 2% = 40
- 1 000 × 4% = 40
- 2 000 × 4% = 80

Message:
> 2× relevant traffic × 2× conversion = 4× actions

Must be labelled as a hypothetical mathematical example, not a forecast or customer result.

Alternative site-level visual:
> Bli funnet → bli valgt → gå videre → mål verdi

Explain that improvements in one part can make another part more valuable.

## Language rules

Avoid the word “dyrt” in package positioning.
Talk about:
- value;
- complexity;
- capacity;
- simultaneity;
- number of customer journeys;
- what must improve together.

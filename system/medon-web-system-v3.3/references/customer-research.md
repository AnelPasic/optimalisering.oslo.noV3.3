# Customer Research Reference

Prioritize evidence in this order when available:
- actual sales/support calls;
- customer emails;
- CRM notes;
- reviews;
- Search Console/GBP queries;
- onsite search;
- survey/user-test data;
- session behavior;
- relevant forums/communities;
- competitor material.

Extract:
- trigger event;
- desired outcome;
- language used for the problem;
- alternatives tried;
- anxieties/objections;
- reasons to choose;
- reasons to delay;
- moments where current process fails.

Do not elevate one anecdote into market truth. Record segment/frequency/recency/confidence where material.

# Assessment / Diagnostic Funnel Pattern

Optional. Use when the customer does not fully understand the problem or when diagnosis creates real value before the sale.

## Good assessment behavior

The assessment should:
- reveal a real problem/gap;
- provide immediate useful insight;
- collect only qualification data that changes the result or next step;
- route the visitor to the smallest sensible next step.

It must not be a disguised long contact form.

## Result-first principle

Deliver value before the main sales ask.

A useful result page may show:
- status/result;
- 2–4 concrete observations;
- prioritized improvements;
- why they matter;
- an appropriate next step.

Do not hide the entire result behind “book a call”.

## Dynamic routing

Possible outcomes:
- high fit / urgent / clear need → audit, meeting or offer;
- medium fit → smaller paid/free next step, workshop, guide or nurture;
- low fit / poor timing → relevant content or no-pressure follow-up.

## Question discipline

Ask only what improves:
- diagnosis;
- recommendation;
- qualification;
- routing.

Phone may be optional until intent justifies it.

## First-party research loop

Aggregated/anonymized assessment data can improve:
- offers;
- copy;
- FAQs;
- ads;
- service demand understanding;
- future content.

Handle all personal data under the project's privacy rules.

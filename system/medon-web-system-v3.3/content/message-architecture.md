## 10. Process message

Explain only the amount of process needed to make the service feel understandable and achievable. Do not force an arbitrary number of steps.

## 11. Section-order rationale

Order sections by visitor awareness and traffic intent, not a universal template.

Examples:
- high-intent search → offer/proof/action earlier;
- colder interruption traffic → more context/problem recognition first;
- complex B2B → more process/risk explanation;
- branded traffic → less category education.

## 12. Closing argument

For visitors who needed more information before acting:
- concise outcome recap;
- strongest remaining proof;
- final objection/risk reducer;
- clear CTA;
- what happens next.

## Approval test

Before design:
- Can the argument work as plain text?
- Is the buyer obvious?
- Is the desired outcome obvious?
- Is the primary action obvious?
- Does the proof answer real doubt?
- Does the page have a point of view/differentiator?

If the answer is no, do not solve it with visuals.

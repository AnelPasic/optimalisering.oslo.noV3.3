# Buyer Certainty Gap

A commercial page should reveal what the visitor still needs to believe before acting.

Check:
- Can this work for my situation?
- How does it work?
- Who has it worked for?
- What evidence exists?
- What does it cost / what drives price?
- What exactly do I get?
- What happens after I act?
- What are the risks/commitments?
- How long until useful value?
- Who is responsible for what?

## Output

Do not output a fake universal “certainty score”.

Output:

```text
UNCERTAINTY:
Evidence currently available:
Gap:
Best resolution:
Where on page:
```

Use this alongside Value Equation and objections research.

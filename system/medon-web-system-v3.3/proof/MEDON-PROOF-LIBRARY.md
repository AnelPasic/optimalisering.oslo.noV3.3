## Publication contract

Store evidence, wording/review and permission states separately. Allowed factual evidence states may include `VERIFIED_FIRST_PARTY`, `VERIFIED_EXTERNAL` and `CLIENT_CONFIRMED`, but they still require scoped wording and relevant publication permission.

`SUPPORTABLE`, `OBSERVED`, `INFERENCE`, `UNKNOWN`, `STALE` and `DO_NOT_PUBLISH` are not automatic production approval states.

For performance proof record, where applicable:
- proof ID/customer/industry;
- public naming/media permission;
- service/channel/business goal;
- metric definition/unit/denominator;
- baseline/result periods and values;
- absolute and relative change;
- actual intervention + dates;
- source/snapshot/checked date;
- measurement continuity;
- attribution confidence;
- external factors;
- evidence/review/permission status;
- exact approved wording + allowed locations;
- expiry/recheck + approver/date.

Unknown fields remain unknown. Do not fill them with plausible defaults.

## Arithmetic / attribution rules

- Relative change: `(result - baseline) / baseline × 100%` when definitions are comparable and baseline is non-zero.
- For rates, report percentage-point difference and relative difference correctly.
- Aggregated CPL = comparable total spend / comparable valid leads; do not average monthly CPLs unweighted.
- ROAS is revenue/spend on the stated basis; it is not automatically ROI/profit.
- Before/after association does not automatically prove isolated causality.
- Tracking repairs can raise reported conversion counts without equivalent business growth.
- Include relevant spend, traffic, lead quality, seasonality, offer and measurement changes where material.

## Presentation after approval

Use the most relevant claim, not the largest percentage. Preferred compact form:

```text
customer / industry
→ starting point
→ actual intervention
→ measured result
→ period / source / context / limitation
```

Route conversion proof beside conversion claims, visibility proof beside visibility, paid proof beside paid work and measurement/reconciliation proof beside measurement claims.

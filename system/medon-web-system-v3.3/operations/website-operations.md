# Website Operations After Launch

A commercial website is a system, not a one-off deliverable.

The point is not to invent recurring work. The point is to keep facts, conversions and visibility healthy and improve what evidence says matters.

## Ownership

Before handoff define who owns:
- content truth / service/pricing changes;
- proof/case/review freshness;
- forms/integrations;
- lead routing;
- analytics/conversions;
- search/indexation monitoring;
- performance/errors;
- CMS/users/access;
- backups/version history;
- privacy/consent changes.

## Suggested review areas

Cadence depends on customer value/change rate; do not hardcode weekly/monthly work without need.

Review when appropriate:
- broken forms/integrations;
- lead quality and downstream outcomes;
- GSC queries/impressions/indexation;
- crawl/template regressions;
- content decay / outdated claims;
- AI mention/citation/source drift where measured;
- important competitors/new source surfaces;
- offer/package changes;
- real reviews/cases/proof;
- page speed/field issues;
- conversion funnel leaks.

## SEO + paid feedback

Where Ads data exists, compare high-intent paid queries/conversions with organic/content opportunities and landing-page quality. Do not run SEO and paid learning in silos when shared evidence can improve both.

## Commercial retainer principle

Recurring work must have a defined job and output, for example:
- monitor/fix;
- learn/iterate;
- publish/distribute verified evidence;
- improve conversion;
- maintain tracking/integrations.

Do not sell “maintenance” that is just a calendar reminder with no measurable purpose.

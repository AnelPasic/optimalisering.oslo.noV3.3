# Optimalisering Oslo — Design Priors from V2

These are project-specific lessons from prior design iteration. They are **strong priors**, not generic Medon-system defaults and not permission to clone references.

## Strong positive priors
- VisitInvite / InStay: strongest overall feel for comfortable, trustworthy Nordic presentation.
- Mementor: strong commercial/content/product/pricing balance.
- Bring: useful reference for friendly forms, shape language and controlled color/life.
- Typography: Instrument Sans + Figtree is a strong prior from previous testing.
- comfortable whitespace, but not sterile emptiness;
- visible variation in cards/forms/section treatments;
- rounded language used consistently, not giant rounded boxes around everything;
- simple mathematical teaching should remain visually prominent where it adds understanding;
- desktop and mobile must be judged together.

## Negative priors / known failure modes
Avoid:
- pure-white sterile canvas everywhere;
- dull conservative “safe agency” look;
- aggressive ad/landing-page aesthetic;
- dark-blue SaaS/dashboard visual language;
- generic AI gradients/icon clutter;
- uniform cardification of every paragraph;
- changing type, palette, geometry, layout and content simultaneously;
- full-site visual scaling before one representative page works;
- removing the useful service hub/teaching simply to make a cleaner mockup.

## Review rule
If a new study feels wrong, diagnose the failing layer and change the smallest coherent variable. Do not restart the entire design unless explicitly decided.

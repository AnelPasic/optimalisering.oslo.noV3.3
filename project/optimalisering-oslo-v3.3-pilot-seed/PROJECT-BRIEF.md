# Project Brief — Optimalisering Oslo — clean pilot

Updated: 2026-10-08
Mode: greenfield commercial site / STANDARD / MEDON_OWNED
Provider: Medon AS
Service brand/domain: Optimalisering Oslo / optimalisering.oslo.no
Primary market: Norway first; Nordic customers remain eligible, later market-specific portfolios only when justified.

## Business objective
Generate more qualified business enquiries for Medon through a clear, useful optimization offer.

## Core positioning
Diagnose the limiting acquisition/conversion step and improve the highest-value constraint rather than selling activity for activity's sake.

Two market-facing pillars:
1. **Synlighet** — SEO + AI visibility / relevant discovery.
2. **Konvertering** — CRO, landing pages/forms/purchase paths and offer/message friction where relevant.

Paid visibility is supporting/conditional: it can be a speed accelerator when economics justify it. Do not position the service as anti-ads.

Measurement is foundational, not necessarily a headline service.

## Primary buyer
Owner/managing director first. Explain economics, responsibility, cost and next action in plain language with little assumed channel knowledge.

Eligible customer models:
- B2B/lead generation: qualified enquiries → customers/revenue where attribution allows.
- Ecommerce: purchases/revenue plus margin/order economics where relevant.

## Commercial thesis
Illustrative model only:
`BUSINESS OUTPUT ≈ RELEVANT TRAFFIC × CONVERSION RATE × CUSTOMER VALUE`

Use simple math to teach leverage, but never turn arithmetic examples into promised uplift or client proof.

## Primary conversion
Free bounded manual assessment / “gratis sjekk”: short reasoned guidance on what Medon would start with, why, and whether visibility, conversion or both should come first.

It is not an automated score, full audit, complete issue list, forecast or free implementation.

## Brand relationship
Optimalisering Oslo is a relatively autonomous service brand. Medon AS must be clearly identified as owner/provider. Do not invent a separate entity, Oslo office, inherited proof or separate history.

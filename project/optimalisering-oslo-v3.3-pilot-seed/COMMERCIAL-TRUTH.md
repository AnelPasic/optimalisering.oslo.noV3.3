# Commercial Truth — pilot baseline

## KEEP — high confidence
- recurring service model;
- two standard need-based primary services: Visibility and Conversion;
- clearly visible combination path for clients with both constraints;
- larger/custom scope by agreement;
- free bounded manual assessment as primary entry;
- Medon analyzes, implements agreed/appropriate work and follows effects;
- provider prioritizes work based on experience and expected commercial payoff rather than requiring customer approval for every micro-task;
- main website/store is default standard scope; additional sites/landing pages are conditional by need/scope;
- ad budget and applicable external platform costs are outside normal service price unless explicitly included.

## INTERNAL DELIVERY INPUTS — preserve, do not publish as quotas
Owner estimates for each primary standard service:
- first 1–2 months: 5–10 h/customer/month;
- subsequent normal phase: 4–5 h/customer/month;
- after ~1 year: 1–2 h/customer/month as an internal planning estimate, not a retention guarantee or automatic scope/price cut.

Combination planning estimate: `(Visibility + Conversion effort) × 0.75`.
Internal cost input: ~550 NOK/hour including ordinary delivery overhead/tools.
Available delivery capacity input: ~150 h/month when demand exists.

## REVIEW — do not inherit as unquestionable production truth
Old Gate 3 selected price candidates:
- Visibility: 4,490 NOK/month ex. VAT;
- Conversion: 4,490 NOK/month ex. VAT;
- combination: 6,990 NOK/month ex. VAT.

Reason for review: at the supplied internal cost/effort model, early delivery can be underwater:
- primary service cost range first phase: 2,750–5,500 NOK/month;
- combination first phase: 4,125–8,250 NOK/month.

Therefore these prices are **owner-selected historical candidates, not v3.3 production locks** until scope/unit economics are reconciled. Do not silently publish them.

## MISSING / must be resolved before final pricing copy
- finite standard inclusions/exclusions and service boundaries;
- exact contractual/billing/cancellation terms;
- any external-cost wording that needs precise examples;
- whether pricing deliberately subsidizes onboarding/early months and why;
- exact public combination comparison/discount claim after scope validation.
